Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Here are answers to the most common questions about MatchWithIjas.

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration is completely free. Only a one-time Home Verification fee is applicable before accessing verified profiles.

Why is Home Verification required?

Home Verification helps us eliminate fake profiles and build a trusted matchmaking network.

Will everyone be able to see my profile?

No. Your profile is shared only with suitable verified matches.

When will I receive contact details?

After Home Verification, you can view verified profiles and contact details without any additional payment.

Are there any monthly charges?

No. There are no monthly or hidden charges.
